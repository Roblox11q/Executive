Executive Stand Docs

📅 Updated June 2025 ⚡ v2.0

Welcome to the official Executive Stand documentation. This guide covers everything from first-time setup to advanced configuration and deployment.

What is Executive Stand? A subscription-based platform for configuring Roblox Stand scripts across Da Hood and related games. You set up your config once via the web dashboard or Discord bot — your alt accounts pull the config automatically on inject.

How it works

Executive Stand has three layers:

  1. Web Dashboard — configure your stand, manage alts, download your loader (Premium/Bypass only).
  2. Discord Bot — alternative interface for all plans; run commands to configure, add alts, and get your loader.
  3. Lua Scripts — StandLoader.lua (inject on alts) and StandMain.lua (fetched securely at runtime).

Quick Start

⏱ ~5 minutes

Get from zero to injected in five steps.

Step 1 — Log in

Click Login with Discord in the top nav. Authorise the OAuth prompt — we only request your basic profile (identify scope).

Step 2 — Set your Roblox owner

Go to Stand Config in the dashboard. Enter your main Roblox username in the Roblox Owner Username field. This is the account that types commands in public chat — never inject on this account.

Step 3 — Add your alts

Go to Alt Accounts. Enter each alt's Roblox username and assign it a formation slot. Free users can add up to 2 alts; Premium up to 10; Bypass unlimited.

Step 4 — Download your loader

Go to Loader and click Download StandLoader.lua. This file contains your personal config embedded and is unique to your account.

Step 5 — Inject & play

Open your executor on an alt account in a Da Hood server. Execute StandLoader.lua. Switch to your main and type commands in public chat using your prefix (default: .).

⛔ Never inject on your main account. The main account controls alts via public chat. Injecting on your main will cause unexpected behaviour.

Stand Config

The Stand Config page (Premium/Bypass only) lets you edit all settings that get embedded into your loader. Free users configure via Discord bot commands.

Basic settings

  • Owner — your main Roblox username. Commands typed in public chat with your prefix by this user will control your alts.
  • Key — your License Hub key. Required for secure loader delivery.
  • Prefix — the character your main types before commands (default: .). Keep it short and unique.
  • Gun — the Da Hood item name for your preferred weapon. Must match the exact in-game name.
  • Slot — the default formation slot alts join when they spawn.
  • Animation ID — Roblox asset ID for your alt's idle animation.

Toggles

  • Auto Mask — alts automatically equip a mask when available.
  • Auto Armor — alts automatically equip armor on spawn.
  • Muscle — enables custom muscle size for alts.
  • Mute Gun Sounds — suppresses gun sound effects from alts.
  • Infinite Ammo — Bypass only. Alts never run out of ammo.
  • Character Randomizer — Bypass only. Alts get random appearances each spawn.
⚠️ After saving config, download a fresh loader from the Loader page. Already-injected alts will not pick up config changes until re-injected.

Controllers are additional Roblox usernames (besides your owner) that can command your alts. Add them via the Discord bot with /addcontroller or via the config form. Useful if you share control with a friend.

Loader & Inject

The loader is a personalised Lua script generated from your config. It bootstraps StandMain.lua securely at runtime — your config and key are never stored in plaintext on disk.

Downloading your loader

  1. Go to the Loader tab in the dashboard.
  2. Review the preview — confirm your owner name and alts look correct.
  3. Click Download StandLoader.lua.
  4. Re-download after every config change.

Injection steps

  1. Join a supported game on your alt account.
  2. Open your Roblox executor.
  3. Load StandLoader.lua and execute it.
  4. Wait for the confirmation output in your executor console.
  5. Switch to your main account and issue commands using your prefix.
⛔ NEVER inject the loader on your main account.

Secure delivery

The loader makes an authenticated request to the Render backend to fetch StandMain.lua at runtime. Your license key is verified server-side before the script is returned. This means the full stand source is never embedded in a file sitting on your disk.

License Keys

License keys authenticate your stand against the backend. Each user has one key tied to their Discord ID.

Getting a key

Keys are assigned automatically when you subscribe via Stripe, or manually by staff after purchase. You can view your current key in Stand Config → License Key.

Updating your key

If your key is compromised or needs rotation, use the Discord bot command /setkey or update it in the Config page and re-download your loader.

Key sharing

⛔ Never share your key. Keys are tied to your Discord ID. Sharing a key with another user may result in both accounts being permanently banned without refund.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't I access the dashboard editor?

The web dashboard config editor requires a Premium or Bypass plan. Free users can configure everything via Discord bot slash commands. See Bot Commands.

My loader doesn't work after I changed my config.

Config changes don't update an already-injected loader. After saving config, go to the Loader tab, re-download, and re-inject on your alt.

The alts aren't responding to my commands.

  • Make sure you're typing the command on your main (owner) account, not your alts.
  • Confirm the owner username in config exactly matches your Roblox username (case-insensitive).
  • Check the Stand status in our Discord — if it's 🔴 Down or 🔵 Updating, /loader will be blocked.
  • Try re-injecting the loader on the alt.

My alts are killing each other / killing me.

Make sure Friendly Fire Prevention is enabled in Stand Config → Toggles. Then re-download your loader and re-inject on all alts. The friend list is built from your owner, alts, and controllers — all must be linked in the dashboard before generating the loader. See the Friendly Fire docs for full details.

Can I use the same loader on multiple alts?

Yes. The same StandLoader.lua can be injected on all your alts simultaneously. They each connect to the same config and obey commands from the same owner.

I got banned — can I appeal?

Open a Billing ticket in our Discord server. Bans for key sharing and chargebacks are generally permanent. Other bans may be appealed within 7 days.

Does Executive Stand work on Mac / mobile?

The web dashboard works on any device. Roblox exploit injection requires a Windows PC with a compatible executor — that is unrelated to Executive Stand itself.

Friendly Fire Prevention

⚔️ Keeping your alts from attacking each other

By default, Da Hood alts injected with the same stand can accidentally target and kill each other if their combat AI is active. Executive Stand prevents this using a friendly-fire ignore list built from your config.

How it works

When your loader is generated, it embeds the usernames of all your linked alts plus your owner account into a protected FriendList table inside the script. The combat module checks this list before firing at any player — if the target's username is in the list, the alt skips them entirely.

-- Automatically generated in your StandLoader.lua:
StandConfig.FriendList = {
    "YourOwnerUsername",   -- always included
    "AltOne",
    "AltTwo",
    "AltThree",
    -- controllers are included too
}

-- Combat check (inside StandMain.lua):
local function IsFriend(name)
    for _, v in ipairs(StandConfig.FriendList) do
        if v:lower() == name:lower() then
            return true
        end
    end
    return false
end

-- Before any attack action:
if IsFriend(target.Name) then return end

What's protected

  • Your owner username — always added automatically.
  • All linked alts from the Alt Accounts tab.
  • All controllers from the Controllers tab.
The friend list is case-insensitive. AltOne and altone are treated the same.

Adding extra friends

If you're running with a friend who has their own stand, you can add their username as a Controller so your alts ignore them too — even if they're not part of your config.

Re-download after changes

⚠️ Important: The friend list is embedded at loader generation time. Any time you add or remove an alt or controller, you must re-download your loader from the Loader tab and re-inject on all alts for the updated list to take effect.

Troubleshooting

  • Alts still attacking each other — you're running an old loader. Re-download and re-inject.
  • Alt attacking owner — make sure the Owner field in Stand Config matches your exact Roblox username. Even a trailing space will break the match.
  • Alt attacking a controller — add the controller in the Controllers tab, save, re-download loader, re-inject.
